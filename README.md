# Locate Me

> [!WARNING]
> Locate Me should not be used in situations where you are in danger. Locate Me may be inaccurate.
> Do not solely rely on Locate Me to navigate unfamiliar terrain.

> [!NOTE]
> Locate Me, due to using Ordnance Survey grid references, only works in the UK.

Locate Me is a compass app that shows you:

- Your OS Grid Reference
- A compass
- Bearing and pitch
- Height
